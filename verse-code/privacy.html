What we collect

When you sign up through our Facebook or Instagram form, we receive the email address you enter and the date you signed up. We do not ask for your religious beliefs, and we do not record them.

Why we use it

We use your email address only to tell you when the game launches. We will send one launch email. We will not sell your email address or use it for anything else.

We rely on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time by emailing us or using the unsubscribe link in our email.

Who else handles it

  • Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. runs the signup form on Facebook and Instagram. Meta's own privacy policy covers what Meta collects when you use its services.
  • Our email provider sends the launch email on our behalf, under a data processing agreement.
  • GitHub hosts this page and may log technical data, such as your IP address, when you visit it.

Some of these providers may process data outside the EU. Where they do, they rely on safeguards such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until we send the launch email, or until you ask us to delete it. If we decide not to launch either game, we will delete the list. In any case, we will delete it by 31 December 2027.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive a copy of it. To use any of these rights, email support@dropdeck.cc.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. Ours is the Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
