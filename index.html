.Services.

Five ways to bring in senior production, from a two-week tune-up to months embedded in your team.

  1. 01

    Embedded Senior Producer

    Join your senior team for weeks or months. Cover leave or a hiring gap, or add senior weight for a milestone.

    Good for: studios short a senior producer.

  2. 02

    Project Rescue

    Work out what's off track, cut scope, restructure the team and get it shipping again.

    Good for: projects heading for a missed date.

  3. 03

    Co-dev & Partner Management

    Run external teams, outsourcers and vendors so they deliver what you actually need, on time.

    Good for: studios working with co-dev partners or outsourcing.

  4. 04

    Production Tune-Up

    A health check of how your team plans, tracks and ships, to find where work stalls. Then I set up or tune the systems that keep it moving.

    Good for: teams whose releases keep slipping, or studios that have outgrown how they work.

  5. 05

    Coaching & Workshops

    Mentoring for producers and new leads. Kickoffs and planning resets for whole teams.

    Good for: studios growing their production team.

Not sure which fits? Tell me what's stuck and I'll suggest where to start.

The Cycle: Frontier · YAGER

Shipping the final season with a third of the team

Most of the studio moved to co-dev projects, which left about 30 of 100 developers for the last season. I split them into small strike teams, cut scope down to what players valued most, and moved trailers, music, animation and art to outsourcing partners. We shipped on schedule.

Days → hours

SEED · KLANG Games

Cutting release delays

Releases were running days late. Working with a data analyst, I went through the studio's Jira and pull request history, found four patterns behind the delays, and set up an early-warning dashboard and a risk register.

6 promotions

YAGER · Direct reports

Growing people, not just teams

At peak, I managed about 20 people across three teams. Beyond the day-to-day work, I sharpened their soft skills with a personal touch and pushed them to grow as people, not just in their roles.
